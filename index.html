3C. Interactive Offline Suro & Boyo Strategy Cards

Click on strategy cards to inspect tips and apply them to your notes below:

CARD 1 — Dialogue & Tone
  • Use contractions (I'm, didn't) to sound natural.
  • Add quick reactions: Sura sighed, "Fine, let's divide it."
  • Show feelings through action rather than telling ("I am mad").
CARD 2 — Setting & Sensory Details
  • Add one vivid sensory detail (e.g., tense silence, crowded hall).
  • Use specific descriptors instead of generic words.
CARD 3 — Temporal Conjunctions
  • Start key sentences with time markers: Suddenly, After that, Eventually.
  • Avoid repeating "then" continuously.
CARD 4 — Meaningful Resolution
  • End with what the characters learned about each other.
  • Connect the resolution directly to character growth and respect.

"I've decided to run for President!"
"Good luck! But I will win!"
During campaign week...
"My academic program is best!"
"My sports plan is stronger!"
Election day arrived...
"Why are the votes so close?"
"Neither of us will back down!"
Tied votes! SPLASH!
"A tied vote? Now what?!"
"We can't keep arguing forever!"
SPLASH!
After thoughtful discussion...
"What if we share presidency?"
"You handle academics, I handle events!"
"Teamwork beats rivalry!"
"We are better together!"
